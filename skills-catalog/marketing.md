# Skills Catalog — Marketing & Social

Skills for planning, writing, and analyzing social media content. Two
sources: the `-sms` suffixed pack from
[blacktwist/social-media-skills](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills)
(text-first platforms — install with `npx skills add
blacktwist/social-media-skills`), and three from the much larger
[social-media-skills/skills](https://github.com/social-media-skills/skills)
collection (106 skills covering strategy, writing, video, and analytics
across all platforms). Unlike the design categories above, most of these
expect ongoing use on a specific account/brand rather than a one-off task —
see **Social Media Context** below, which the rest of the blacktwist pack is
built to read from.

### Foundational setup

#### [Social Media Context](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/social-media-context-sms/SKILL.md)
Sets up `.agents/social-media-context-sms.md` — brand voice, audience,
content pillars, and platform preferences — that every other skill in the
blacktwist pack reads from. Run this first on a new project so the rest of
the pack doesn't need the same brief repeated per task. Already set up on
one active project (context file created 2026-08-14); no published content
yet, so no worked-example writeup in `applications/` yet — add one once real
posts exist to show the pack's actual output.

### Strategy & planning

#### [Content Strategy](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/content-strategy-sms/SKILL.md)
Plans the *what* and *why* of posting — topic clusters, content pillars,
content mix — before anything gets written.

#### [Content Calendar](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/content-calendar-sms/SKILL.md)
Turns a strategy into a posting schedule — cadence, batching, what goes out
when.

#### [Platform Strategy](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/platform-strategy-sms/SKILL.md)
Platform-specific tactical guidance for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Threads, and
Bluesky — algorithm behavior, cross-posting tradeoffs, which platform to
prioritize.

### Writing

#### [Post Writer](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/post-writer-sms/SKILL.md)
Writes a single post for any of LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Threads, Bluesky,
Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, or YouTube Community.

#### [Thread Writer](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/thread-writer-sms/SKILL.md)
Writes multi-part threads/series — Twitter threads, LinkedIn multi-part
posts, Reel/Story series, YouTube video series.

#### [Carousel Writer](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/carousel-writer-sms/SKILL.md)
Writes slide-by-slide text content for swipeable formats — LinkedIn/
Instagram/Facebook carousels, TikTok photo carousels, Pinterest Idea Pins.
Text content only, not visual design (pair with a design skill for the
slides themselves).

#### [Caption Writer](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/caption-writer-sms/SKILL.md)
Writes captions for visual-first posts (Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest,
YouTube) — the words that go with an image/video you already have.

#### [Hook Writer](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/hook-writer-sms/SKILL.md)
Writes opening lines/hooks — first sentences, video hooks, thumbnail text,
pin titles. Can be invoked standalone or called by the writing skills above
when a post needs a stronger opener.

#### [Content Repurposer](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/content-repurposer-sms/SKILL.md)
Adapts one piece of content across formats and platforms — blog to social,
newsletter to posts, video to clips, podcast to Reels.

#### [Short-Form Video Script](https://github.com/social-media-skills/skills/blob/main/skills/short-form-video-script/SKILL.md)
Scripts vertical video (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) using a retention-focused
framework (hook, pacing, loop, sound-off captions). Produces the script
(spoken lines, on-screen text, shot direction) — filming/editing stays
manual. From the larger `social-media-skills/skills` collection, not the
blacktwist pack — routes platform specifics to sibling skills
(`reels-script`, `tiktok-script`, `youtube-shorts`) in that same repo.

### Analytics & optimization

#### [Performance Analyzer](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/performance-analyzer-sms/SKILL.md)
Analyzes post performance — engagement, impressions, best posts, why a
specific post underperformed.

#### [Audience Growth Tracker](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/audience-growth-tracker-sms/SKILL.md)
Tracks follower growth and what's driving (or losing) followers.

#### [Content Pattern Analyzer](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/content-pattern-analyzer-sms/SKILL.md)
Finds patterns across content — best topics/formats/times — to identify
what to do more or less of.

#### [Optimization Advisor](https://github.com/blacktwist/social-media-skills/blob/main/skills/optimization-advisor-sms/SKILL.md)
Synthesizes performance + audience + pattern analysis into prioritized next
actions. The rollup skill — use after the three analyzers above, not
instead of them.

### Research

#### [Competitor Analysis](https://github.com/social-media-skills/skills/blob/main/skills/competitor-analysis/SKILL.md)
Public-data competitive reconnaissance — positioning, content pillars,
formats, cadence — to find a content gap to own. From the larger
`social-media-skills/skills` collection. Distinct from **Viral
Reverse-Engineering** below (account-level landscape vs. single-post
teardown).

#### [Viral Reverse-Engineering](https://github.com/social-media-skills/skills/blob/main/skills/viral-reverse-engineering/SKILL.md)
Breaks down why a specific post/video overperformed and extracts the
repeatable mechanism, with a check for whether it's actually replicable in
your own niche (flags virality drivers that aren't, e.g. being a huge
account already). From the larger `social-media-skills/skills` collection.
