# Skills Catalog — Marketing & Social

Skills for planning, writing, and analyzing social media content. Installed
as a set (the `-sms` suffix marks the pack) alongside a few standalone
research/writing skills that cover adjacent ground. Unlike the design
categories above, most of these expect ongoing use on a specific
account/brand rather than a one-off task — see **Social Media Context**
below, which the rest of the set is built to read from.

### Foundational setup

#### Social Media Context
Sets up `.agents/social-media-context-sms.md` — brand voice, audience,
content pillars, and platform preferences — that every other skill in this
set reads from. Run this first on a new project so the rest of the pack
doesn't need the same brief repeated per task. Already set up on one active
project (context file created 2026-08-14); no published content yet, so no
worked-example writeup in `applications/` yet — add one once real posts
exist to show the pack's actual output.

### Strategy & planning

#### Content Strategy
Plans the *what* and *why* of posting — topic clusters, content pillars,
content mix — before anything gets written.

#### Content Calendar
Turns a strategy into a posting schedule — cadence, batching, what goes out
when.

#### Platform Strategy
Platform-specific tactical guidance for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Threads, and
Bluesky — algorithm behavior, cross-posting tradeoffs, which platform to
prioritize.

### Writing

#### Post Writer
Writes a single post for any of LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Threads, Bluesky,
Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, or YouTube Community.

#### Thread Writer
Writes multi-part threads/series — Twitter threads, LinkedIn multi-part
posts, Reel/Story series, YouTube video series.

#### Carousel Writer
Writes slide-by-slide text content for swipeable formats — LinkedIn/
Instagram/Facebook carousels, TikTok photo carousels, Pinterest Idea Pins.
Text content only, not visual design (pair with a design skill for the
slides themselves).

#### Caption Writer
Writes captions for visual-first posts (Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest,
YouTube) — the words that go with an image/video you already have.

#### Hook Writer
Writes opening lines/hooks — first sentences, video hooks, thumbnail text,
pin titles. Can be invoked standalone or called by the writing skills above
when a post needs a stronger opener.

#### Content Repurposer
Adapts one piece of content across formats and platforms — blog to social,
newsletter to posts, video to clips, podcast to Reels.

#### Short-Form Video Script
Scripts vertical video (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) using a retention-focused
framework (hook, pacing, loop, sound-off captions). Produces the script
(spoken lines, on-screen text, shot direction) — filming/editing stays
manual.

### Analytics & optimization

#### Performance Analyzer
Analyzes post performance — engagement, impressions, best posts, why a
specific post underperformed.

#### Audience Growth Tracker
Tracks follower growth and what's driving (or losing) followers.

#### Content Pattern Analyzer
Finds patterns across content — best topics/formats/times — to identify
what to do more or less of.

#### Optimization Advisor
Synthesizes performance + audience + pattern analysis into prioritized next
actions. The rollup skill — use after the three analyzers above, not
instead of them.

### Research

#### Competitor Analysis
Public-data competitive reconnaissance — positioning, content pillars,
formats, cadence — to find a content gap to own. Distinct from
**Viral Reverse-Engineering** below (account-level landscape vs. single-post
teardown).

#### Viral Reverse-Engineering
Breaks down why a specific post/video overperformed and extracts the
repeatable mechanism, with a check for whether it's actually replicable in
your own niche (flags virality drivers that aren't, e.g. being a huge
account already).
